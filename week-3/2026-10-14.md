# q Daily Quiz — 2026-10-14 (Wednesday)

Theme: lists & sets. 15 quick challenges, easy to hard. All solutions fit in 1-3 lines.

---

### 1. Is palindrome number

Check whether an integer's digits form a palindrome.

	q)isPalindromeNumber 12321
	1b
	q)isPalindromeNumber 12345
	0b

---

### 2. Interleave two lists

Interleave two equal-length lists into one, alternating elements.

	q)interleaveTwoLists[1 3 5;2 4 6]
	1 2 3 4 5 6

---

### 3. Remove zeros

Remove all zero values from a list.

	q)removeZeros 1 0 2 0 3 0 4
	1 2 3 4

---

### 4. Count above threshold

Count how many elements of a vector are greater than a given value.

	q)countAbove[3 1 4 1 5 9 2 6;4]
	3

---

### 5. Index of max

Return the index of the maximum value in a vector.

	q)argMax 3 1 4 1 5 9 2 6
	5

---

### 6. Index of min

Return the index of the minimum value in a vector.

	q)argMin 3 1 4 1 5 9 2 6
	1

---

### 7. Kth smallest

Return the kth smallest element of a vector.

	q)kthSmallest[3 1 4 1 5 9 2 6;3]
	2

---

### 8. Is permutation of

Check whether two lists are permutations of each other (same elements, same multiplicities).

	q)isPermutationOf[1 2 3;3 2 1]
	1b
	q)isPermutationOf[1 2 3;1 2 2]
	0b

---

### 9. Count in range

Count how many elements of a vector fall within a given [lo,hi] range.

	q)countInRange[1 5 10 15 20;5;15]
	3

---

### 10. Top N values

Return the n largest values in a vector.

	q)topN[3 1 4 1 5 9 2 6;3]
	9 6 5

---

### 11. Bottom N values

Return the n smallest values in a vector.

	q)bottomN[3 1 4 1 5 9 2 6;3]
	1 1 2

---

### 12. Is consecutive

Check whether a vector is a run of consecutive integers increasing by 1.

	q)isAscByOne 5 6 7 8
	1b
	q)isAscByOne 5 6 8 9
	0b

---

### 13. Cartesian product

Return every pair formed by combining elements of two lists (hint: there's a built-in for this).

	q)1 2 cross 10 20
	1 10
	1 20
	2 10
	2 20

---

### 14. Sum of unique values

Sum the distinct values in a vector (each value counted once, however many times it occurs).

	q)sumUnique 1 2 2 3 3 3
	6

---

### 15. Count occurrences

Count how many times a value appears in a list.

	q)countOccurrences[1 2 3 2 2 4;2]
	3
