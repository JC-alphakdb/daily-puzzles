# q Daily Quiz — 2026-10-15 (Thursday)

Theme: matrices, vectors & stats. 15 quick challenges, easy to hard. All solutions fit in 1-3 lines.

---

### 1. Identity matrix

Build an n×n identity matrix.

	q)identityMatrix 3
	1 0 0
	0 1 0
	0 0 1

---

### 2. Column sums

Sum each column of a matrix (hint: think about what `sum` does to a list of row-lists).

	q)columnSums (1 2 3;4 5 6)
	5 7 9

---

### 3. Row sums

Sum each row of a matrix.

	q)rowSums (1 2 3;4 5 6)
	6 15

---

### 4. Scalar multiply

Multiply every element of a matrix by a scalar.

	q)scalarMultiply[(1 2;3 4);2]
	2 4
	6 8

---

### 5. Rotate a matrix

Rotate a matrix 90 degrees.

	q)rotate90 (1 2;3 4)
	2 4
	1 3

---

### 6. Anti-diagonal sum

Sum the elements on a square matrix's anti-diagonal (top-right to bottom-left).

	q)antiDiagonalSum (1 2 3;4 5 6;7 8 9)
	15

---

### 7. Weighted average

Compute the weighted average of a list of values given their weights.

	q)weightedAverage[1 2 3;1 1 2]
	2.25

---

### 8. Harmonic mean

Compute the harmonic mean of a vector.

	q)harmonicMean 1 2 4
	1.714286

---

### 9. Geometric mean

Compute the geometric mean of a vector.

	q)geometricMean 1 3 9
	3

---

### 10. Age category

Classify an age as a child, teen, or adult.

	q)ageCategory 10
	`child
	q)ageCategory 16
	`teen
	q)ageCategory 30
	`adult

### 11. Grade from score

Convert a numeric score to a letter grade (hint: there's a built-in that does a sorted lookup for you).

	`B

### 13. Sum of primes

Sum all the prime numbers below n.

	q)sumOfPrimesUpTo 20

### 14. Sum of even Fibonacci numbers
