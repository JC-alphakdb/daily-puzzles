# q Daily Quiz — 2026-10-16 (Friday)

Theme: dates, dicts, tables & parsing. 15 quick challenges, easy to hard. All solutions fit in 1-3 lines.

---

### 1. Extract date parts

Return a date's year, month and day as a 3-element list.

	q)extractDateParts 2024.07.15
	2024 7 15

---

### 2. Add days

Add n days to a date.

	q)addDays[2024.01.01;10]
	2024.01.11

---

### 3. Quarter of year

Return which quarter (1-4) a date falls in.

	q)quarterOf 2024.07.15
	3

---

### 4. Days in month

Return how many days are in the month of a given date.

	q)daysInMonth 2024.02.01
	29
	q)daysInMonth 2024.04.01
	30

---

### 5. Count leap years in range

Count how many leap years fall within a range of years (inclusive).

	q)countLeapYearsInRange[2000;2024]
	7

---

### 6. Merge two dictionaries

Merge two dictionaries; where keys clash, the second dictionary's value wins.

	q)mergeDicts[`a`b!1 2;`b`c!30 3]
	a| 1
	b| 30
	c| 3

---

For the next three, assume this table:

	q)t:([] sym:`a`b`a; price:10 20 30)

### 7. Average by group

Write a query that returns the average price for each symbol.

	q)select avg price by sym from t
	sym price
	---------
	a   20
	b   20

### 8. Sort descending

Sort the table by price, descending (hint: there's a built-in for this).

	q)xdesc[`price;t]
	sym price
	---------
	a   30
	b   20
	a   10

### 9. Distinct values

Return the distinct symbols in the table.

	q)exec distinct sym from t
	`a`b

### 10. Parse a comma-separated list

Parse a comma-separated string of numbers into an integer vector.

	q)stringToInts "1,22,333"
