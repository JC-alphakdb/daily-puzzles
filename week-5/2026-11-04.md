# q Daily Quiz — 2026-11-04 (Wednesday)

Theme: joining across three tables. 10 harder challenges spanning customers, products and the orders linking them. Write each answer as a function that takes the stated arguments and returns the result. Most solutions still fit in a handful of lines.

---

Assume these tables for every question today:

	q)customers:([custId:101 102 103] name:`Alice`Bob`Carol; city:`NYC`LA`NYC)
	q)customers
	custId| name  city
	------| ----------
	101   | Alice NYC 
	102   | Bob   LA  
	103   | Carol NYC 

	q)products:([prodId:`P1`P2`P3] pname:`Widget`Gadget`Gizmo; unitPrice:10 25 15f)
	q)products
	prodId| pname  unitPrice
	------| ----------------
	P1    | Widget 10       
	P2    | Gadget 25       
	P3    | Gizmo  15       

	q)orders:([] orderId:1 2 3 4 5; custId:101 102 101 103 102; prodId:`P1`P2`P3`P1`P1; qty:3 2 1 5 4)
	q)orders
	orderId custId prodId qty
	-------------------------
	1       101    P1     3  
	2       102    P2     2  
	3       101    P3     1  
	4       103    P1     5  
	5       102    P1     4  

Both `customers` and `products` are keyed tables.
