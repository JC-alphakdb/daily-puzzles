Theme: trade blotter analytics. 10 harder challenges built around one shared table — each digs a little deeper into the same trade data than the last. Most solutions still fit in a handful of lines.

### 1. VWAP per symbol

Compute the size-weighted average price for each symbol.

	q)select vwap:size wavg price by sym from trades
	sym | vwap
	----| --------
	AAPL| 190.2667
	GOOG| 140.9538
	MSFT| 402.3

### 2. Price of the biggest trade per symbol

For each symbol, return the price of its largest trade (by size).

	q)select price by sym from trades where size=(max;size) fby sym
	sym | price
	----| -----
	AAPL| 190.1
	GOOG| 140.8
	MSFT| 402.3

### 3. Which symbols are net long

Using `side` (`B`=buy, `S`=sell), compute each symbol's net signed size (buys positive, sells negative), then return only the symbols that come out net long.

	q)exec sym from (select net:sum size*1-2*side=`S by sym from trades) where net>0
	`AAPL`MSFT

---

### 4. Buy/sell count imbalance

Return a dictionary mapping each symbol to its number of buy trades minus its number of sell trades.

	q)exec (sum side=`B)-sum side=`S by sym from trades
	AAPL| 1
	GOOG| -2
	MSFT| 1

### 5. Price range per symbol, widest first

Compute each symbol's price range (max price − min price), returned as a dictionary sorted from widest to narrowest.

	q)desc exec (max price)-(min price) by sym from trades
	AAPL| 1.5
	GOOG| 0.4
	MSFT| 0

### 6. Symbols trading above the overall average

Return the symbols whose average price is higher than the average price across *all* trades.

	q)exec sym from (select avgPrice:avg price by sym from trades) where avgPrice > avg trades`price
	,`MSFT

### 7. Notional by side

Compute the total notional value (price × size) traded on each side.

	q)select notional:sum price*size by side from trades

	S   | 46974

### 8. Rank trades within each symbol

Add a `szRank` column: within each symbol, 1 marks the largest trade by size, 2 the next largest, and so on.

	q)update szRank:1+rank neg size by sym from trades
	sym  price size side szRank
	---------------------------
	AAPL 189.5 100  B    3
	GOOG 141.2 50   S    2
	AAPL 190.1 200  B    1

	AAPL 191   150  S    2

### 9. Symbols ranked by notional

Return the symbols ordered from largest to smallest total notional traded.

	q)exec sym from `notional xdesc select notional:sum price*size by sym from trades

### 10. Symbols trading on both sides

Return the symbols that have at least one buy trade *and* at least one sell trade.

	q)exec distinct sym from trades where (sym in exec sym from trades where side=`B) and sym in exec sym from trades where side=`S
	,`AAPL
