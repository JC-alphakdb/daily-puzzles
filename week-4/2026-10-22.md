### 10. Missing data

Find the sensor/10-minute-bucket combinations where that sensor produced no reading at all.

	q)tm:`minute$readings[`time]
	q)buckets:asc distinct 10 xbar tm
	q)sens:asc distinct readings[`sensor]
	q)cp:sens cross buckets
	q)allCombos:([] sensor:cp[;0]; bucket:cp[;1])
	q)existing:select distinct sensor,bucket:10 xbar time.minute from readings
	q)allCombos except existing
	sensor bucket
	-------------
	A      09:20
	B      09:00
	B      09:40
