# q Daily Quiz — 2026-10-21 (Wednesday)

Theme: joining tables. 10 harder challenges across two small related tables — employees and the departments they belong to. Write each answer as a function that takes the stated arguments and returns the result. Most solutions still fit in a handful of lines.

---

Assume these tables for every question today:

	q)employees:([] id:1001 1002 1003 1004 1005; name:`Alice`Bob`Carol`Dave`Eve; deptId:10 20 10 30 20; salary:75000 68000 82000 91000 72000)
	q)employees
	id   name  deptId salary
	------------------------
	1001 Alice 10     75000
	1002 Bob   20     68000
	1003 Carol 10     82000
	1004 Dave  30     91000
	1005 Eve   20     72000

	q)departments:([deptId:10 20 30 40] deptName:`Engineering`Sales`Finance`HR; budget:500000 300000 200000 150000)
	q)departments
	deptId| deptName    budget
	------| ------------------
	10    | Engineering 500000
	20    | Sales       300000
	30    | Finance     200000
	40    | HR          150000

Note `departments` is keyed on `deptId` and not every department has employees.
