# q Daily Quiz — 2026-11-25 (Wednesday)

Theme: project timesheets & functional queries. 10 harder challenges built around people, projects and two weeks of logged hours. Many of today's functions receive *column names as arguments*, so you can't hard-code them in a q-sql template — that's what the functional forms `?[t;c;b;a]` (select/exec) and `![t;c;b;a]` (update/delete) are for (`parse` is your friend for discovering the shape). Write each answer as a function that takes the stated arguments and returns the result. Most solutions still fit in a handful of lines.
