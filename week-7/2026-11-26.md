# q Daily Quiz — 2026-11-26 (Thursday)

Theme: delivery routes & time windows. 10 harder challenges built around a handful of delivery stops on a city grid, each with an opening window and a service time, plus the routes two vans are planned to drive. Expect each-prior style pairing of consecutive stops, a scan that carries the clock forward through waits and service, and finally a brute-force search over stop orders. Write each answer as a function that takes `stops`/`routes` (or the stated arguments) and returns the result. Most solutions still fit in a handful of lines.

Stops sit on a street grid at `(x;y)` km, so the distance between two stops is the Manhattan distance `|dx|+|dy|`, and a van needs **4 minutes per km**. A van arriving before `open` waits until `open`; service then takes `service` minutes, after which the van drives on. A stop is *late* if the van gets there after `close`. Every route starts and ends at the depot.
