# q Daily Quiz — 2026-11-24 (Tuesday)

Theme: inventory & reordering. 10 harder challenges built around a keyed stock table whose `demand` column holds a nested 7-day forecast per SKU. Today leans on functional amend (`@` / `.` with a function, deep indexes into a keyed table) and on simulating day by day with `scan`/`over`. Write each answer as a function that takes `stock` (or the stated arguments) and returns the result. Most solutions still fit in a handful of lines.

---

Assume this keyed table for every question today:

	q)stock:([sku:`bolt`nut`washer`screw`hinge] onHand:120 80 300 30 20; rop:60 50 150 30 10; orderQty:200 150 400 100 30; lead:2 3 1 2 4; demand:(30 25 40 35 20 30 25;20 15 10 30 20 15 25;50 60 40 55 45 50 60;10 5 15 10 10 5 10;3 4 2 6 5 3 4))
	q)stock
	sku   | onHand rop orderQty lead demand              
	------| ---------------------------------------------
	bolt  | 120    60  200      2    30 25 40 35 20 30 25
	nut   | 80     50  150      3    20 15 10 30 20 15 25
	washer| 300    150 400      1    50 60 40 55 45 50 60
	screw | 30     30  100      2    10 5  15 10 10 5  10
	hinge | 20     10  30       4    3  4  2  6  5  3  4
