# q Daily Quiz — 2026-11-12 (Thursday)

Theme: order book depth & window joins. 10 harder challenges on a short series of 3-level order book snapshots plus the trades printed in between. The price/size levels live as nested lists inside the table's columns. Write each answer as a function that takes `book`/`trades` (or the stated arguments) and returns the result. Most solutions still fit in a handful of lines.
