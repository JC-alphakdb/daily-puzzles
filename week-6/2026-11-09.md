# q Daily Quiz — 2026-11-09 (Monday)

Theme: banking transactions & fraud flags. 10 harder challenges built around a morning's card transactions for three accounts — from simple per-account totals up to a combined fraud score. Write each answer as a function that takes `txns` (or the stated arguments) and returns the result. Most solutions still fit in a handful of lines.
